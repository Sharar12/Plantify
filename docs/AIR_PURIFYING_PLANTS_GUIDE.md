# Indoor Air Purification & VOC Filtration Guide

Based on the NASA Clean Air Study and botanical biofiltration research.

## 🌿 Top Indoor Air Scrubbers

### 1. Snake Plant (*Sansevieria trifasciata*)
- **Primary Toxins**: Benzene, Formaldehyde, Trichloroethylene, Xylene.
- **Nocturnal Respiration**: Employs Crassulacean Acid Metabolism (CAM), releasing oxygen during nighttime.
- **Best Location**: Bedrooms and enclosed study spaces.

### 2. Peace Lily (*Spathiphyllum*)
- **Primary Toxins**: Ammonia, Benzene, Formaldehyde, Acetone.
- **Air Transpiration**: High transpiration rate naturally humidifies dry, air-conditioned rooms.
- **Placement**: Bathrooms or shaded corners with high humidity.

### 3. Spider Plant (*Chlorophytum comosum*)
- **Primary Toxins**: Carbon monoxide, Xylene, Formaldehyde.
- **Pet Safety**: 100% non-toxic and feline-friendly.
