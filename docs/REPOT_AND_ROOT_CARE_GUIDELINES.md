# Plant Repotting & Rootbound Diagnosis Handbook

Proper repotting cadence prevents root suffocation and nutrient starvation.

## 🩺 Diagnostic Signs of a Rootbound Plant
1. **Drainage Escape**: Dense white feeder roots visibly emerge from pot bottom drainage holes.
2. **Water Bypassing**: Poured water immediately channels down the side of the pot without wetting soil.
3. **Stunted Growth**: Plant stops unfurling new leaves despite regular fertilization.

## 🪴 Upsizing Golden Rules
- **Pot Diameter**: Never upsize by more than 2 to 3 inches in diameter (e.g. 15cm pot -> 18cm pot). Overly large pots retain excess water causing root rot.
- **Root Loosening**: Gently massage tightly coiled bottom root balls before placing into fresh aerated potting mix.
- **Post-Repot Water**: Water thoroughly once to eliminate internal air pockets around the newly positioned root ball.
