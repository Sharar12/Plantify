# Monsoon Botanical Care Handbook (Bangladesh Climate)

The rainy season (June–August) in Bangladesh features 85-95% humidity and overcast skies.

## 🌧 Key Care Directives
1. **Pinch Back Watering**: Evaporation rates plummet during continuous rains. Check top 3 inches of soil before watering.
2. **Fungicide Precaution**: High moisture promotes powdery mildew and leaf blight. Apply neem oil or organic copper fungicide fortnightly.
3. **Elevate Containers**: Ensure nursery pots sit on elevated pot stands so drainage apertures do not sit in standing saucer water.
