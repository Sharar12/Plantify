# Plantify Dispatch & Delivery Quality Protocol

Because live flora are perishable and sensitive to transit turbulence, delivery staff must adhere to this protocol.

## 📦 Packaging Standards
1. **Root Stability**: Pots are wrapped in burlap and tied at the base with jute cord to prevent soil displacement.
2. **Moisture Conservation**: The root ball is watered 4 hours prior to dispatch; plastic wrap seals moisture without suffocating the root crown.
3. **Upright Positioning**: Boxes feature directional "THIS WAY UP" signage and ventilated breathing apertures.

## 🚚 Vehicle Guidelines
- Two-wheel dispatch boxes must not be tilted past 30 degrees.
- Never expose plants to direct sunlight atop delivery bags during hot midday hours (12 PM - 3 PM).
