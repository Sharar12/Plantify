# Plantify Botanical Photography & Media Guidelines

High visual clarity directly dictates customer buying confidence.

## 📸 Photo Requirements
1. **Lighting**: Shoot under bright morning diffused sunlight (no harsh direct glare or flash reflections).
2. **Angle Standards**:
   - Shot 1: Full silhouette showing pot and mature canopy height.
   - Shot 2: Close-up macro of foliage pattern, leaf venation, and stem texture.
   - Shot 3: Root crown / topsoil view showing potting medium.
3. **Specifications**:
   - Aspect Ratio: 1:1 square (1200x1200px minimum)
   - Format: Modern WebP preferred (lossless 85%), JPEG permitted.
   - Max file size: 2MB per image.
