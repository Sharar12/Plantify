# Plant Care & Botanical Taxonomy Guidelines

This reference defines care requirements and environmental thresholds across the Plantify catalog.

## 🌿 1. Snake Plant (*Sansevieria trifasciata*)
- **Light**: Low to bright indirect sunlight.
- **Watering**: Once every 2 weeks in summer; monthly in winter.
- **Toxicity**: Mildly toxic to cats & dogs (saponins).
- **Air Purification**: High (removes benzene, formaldehyde, xylene).

## 🍃 2. Monstera Deliciosa (Swiss Cheese Plant)
- **Light**: Bright indirect sunlight.
- **Watering**: Weekly once top 2-3 inches of soil dry.
- **Humidity**: High (60-80%). Regular misting recommended.
- **Fertilizer**: Balanced NPK 20-20-20 every 4 weeks during growth season.

## 🌸 3. Peace Lily (*Spathiphyllum*)
- **Light**: Low to moderate shaded lighting.
- **Watering**: Keep soil consistently moist but never soggy.
- **Warning Sign**: Drooping leaves directly signal dehydration.
