# Specialist Botanist Q&A Triage Runbook

This guide standardizes how botanical specialists review customer symptom reports and photos.

## 🔬 Triage Severity Matrix
- **Critical (P1 - Within 6 Hours)**: Severe stem mushiness, widespread sudden leaf drop, mealybug / spider mite infesting neighboring plants.
- **Moderate (P2 - Within 24 Hours)**: Yellowing lower leaves, brown tips (fluoride / humidity issue), slow stunted growth.
- **Low (P3 - Within 48 Hours)**: Cosmetic leaf blemishes, fertilizer schedule adjustments, repotting advice.

## 📝 Diagnostic Template
1. Primary Diagnosis (e.g. Overwatering, root rot, fungal leaf spot)
2. Immediate Action (e.g. Stop watering, inspect root system, prune dead foliage)
3. Ongoing Care adjustments (e.g. Relocate to filtered east window)
